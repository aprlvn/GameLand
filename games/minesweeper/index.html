How to play

  • Tap a tile to reveal it. A number shows how many kittens hide in the 8 tiles around it.
  • Flag a tile you suspect hides a kitty: right-click, long-press, or turn on Flag Mode and tap.
  • Your first tap is always safe.
  • Reveal every safe tile to win!

How to Play

Reveal every tile that isn't hiding a kitty 🐾.
