How to play

  • Drag each of the 3 pieces below onto the grid.
  • Fill an entire row or column to clear it and score bonus points.
  • Clearing several lines with one piece scores extra.
  • Once all 3 pieces are placed, a fresh set appears. The game ends when none of your held pieces fit anywhere.
  • Easy plays on a roomier 10×10 board, Medium is the classic 8×8, and Hard shrinks it to 6×6.

How to Play

Clear as many lines as you can before the grid fills up.

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the grid. It must fit fully inside an empty area.
  • Complete a row or column to clear it — the fuller the clear, the bigger the bonus.
  • New pieces appear once all 3 in the tray are placed.
  • It's game over when none of your held pieces fit anywhere on the board.
  • Easy is a roomier 10×10 board, Medium is the classic 8×8, and Hard shrinks it to 6×6.

There's no rotating — plan around the shapes you're given!
