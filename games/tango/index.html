Balance the grid, hearts and stars

Tap a tile to cycle 💗 → ⭐ → empty

How to play

  • Fill every tile with a heart 💗 or a star ⭐.
  • No more than two of the same symbol may touch in a row, in any direction.
  • Each row and column needs an equal number of hearts and stars.
  • A = between two tiles means they must match; a × means they must differ.
  • Nothing is marked right or wrong until you press Check or fill the whole board.

How to Play

Fill every tile with a heart 💗 or a star ⭐ so that:

  • No more than two of the same symbol touch in a row, in any direction.
  • Each row and column ends up with an equal number of hearts and stars.
  • A = badge between two tiles means they must match. A × badge means they must differ.

Nothing is marked right or wrong until you press Check or fill the whole board.

You balanced it!
