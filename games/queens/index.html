One crown per color, none too close

How to play

  • Place exactly one crown 👑 in every row, every column, and every color region.
  • No two crowns may touch, not even diagonally.
  • Tap once for a small × note (handy for tracking eliminations), again for a crown, and once more to clear it.

How to Play

Place one crown 👑 in every row, column, and color region so that:

  • Exactly one crown per row, per column, and per colored region.
  • No two crowns touch, not even diagonally.

Tap a tile once for a small × note (handy for tracking eliminations), tap again for a crown, and once more to clear it.

All crowned!
